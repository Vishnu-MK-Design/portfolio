Project 01

Healthcare Platform Redesign

Improving accessibility and patient experience across the entire care journey — from booking to follow-up.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Timeline
2024
Category
Healthcare · Web App
Platform
Web & Tablet
Hero mockup — patient dashboard
Overview

A calmer, clearer path through care.

The existing platform had grown feature by feature, leaving patients lost in dense menus and clinical jargon. The redesign set out to make the everyday tasks — booking, records, prescriptions — feel obvious and reassuring.

I led research, information architecture, and the full UI system, working closely with clinicians to keep every flow grounded in how care actually happens.

The work

Challenge & Solution

The Challenge

  • Confusing navigation and deep menus
  • Dense medical jargon for patients
  • Inconsistent components across the app
  • Accessibility gaps for older users

The Solution

  • Restructured IA around patient tasks
  • Plain-language content and clear states
  • A unified, accessible design system
  • WCAG-focused contrast and touch targets

Outcome

Faster, friendlier, more accessible.

The redesigned experience cut friction across the core journeys and gave the team a scalable system to build on. Patients could find what they needed without help, and the interface met accessibility standards from the ground up.
