How I work

Structure first, then polish.

Good interfaces start with how people think and what they’re trying to get done. The visual layer comes once the structure is right.

My process
01

Systems thinking

Reusable UI systems — components, spacing and type rules — that keep a product consistent as it grows.

02

Visual clarity

Typography, spacing and hierarchy tuned so the most important thing on a screen is the first thing people see.

03

Problem-led design

Starting from where people get stuck, then removing steps until the flow feels simple.
