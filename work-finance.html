Project 02

AI Finance Dashboard

Making complex financial data easy to understand and act on, with AI-generated insights that surface what matters.

Role

Timeline

Category
SaaS · Dashboard
Platform

Overview

Turning dense data into clear decisions.

Users were drowning in numbers — spreadsheets of transactions with no story. The product needed to answer one question on every screen: "so what should I do next?"

I designed the dashboard, data visualisations, and the AI insight layer, balancing density with clarity so power users and newcomers both felt at home.

The work

Challenge & Solution

The Challenge

  • Overwhelming tables of raw data
  • No clear signal of what to act on
  • Hard to spot spending patterns
  • Insights buried in reports

The Solution

  • Insight-first dashboard hierarchy
  • Clear, focused data visualisations
  • AI summaries that explain the "why"
  • Actionable nudges surfaced in context

Outcome

Clarity that drives action.

By leading with insight instead of raw data, the dashboard helped users make confident decisions faster. Engagement rose as people actually returned to check their AI-generated summaries.
