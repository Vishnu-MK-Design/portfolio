Sunscape Resorts

A resort poster that sells the feeling first, then the facilities, then the offer.

Role
Visual designer
Year
2024
Type
Graphic design · Print poster
Platform
Print
Company
Concept work
The question it answers

Why should I book this resort?

09
Overview

Selling a holiday on one page.

A concept promotional poster for Sunscape Resort, a tropical getaway. The poster had to make someone want to be there, show what the resort offers and push toward booking with a limited-time discount.

I designed the poster layout, the Sunscape logo and the presentation mockup.

The problem

What was getting in the way

  1. A poster has seconds to convincePassers-by won’t stop to read a list of features.
  2. Facilities need to be scannableSuites, spa, gym, yoga, kids’ area and concierge all had to fit without clutter.
  3. The offer must not get lostThe discount and the booking prompt had to stand out from the imagery.
Approach

Designed around a first glance

The layout leads the eye from the feeling, to the details, to the action.

  1. 01FeelDo I want to be there?
  2. 02LearnWhat does it offer?
  3. 03ActHow do I book?
Key decisions

Why it works the way it does

Decision 01

Photography first

A large resort photo takes the top of the poster, with smaller circular photos of the pool and villas adding detail.

Decision 02

A sunny, warm palette

Yellow and orange curves and circles echo the sun in the Sunscape logo and frame the photography.

Decision 03

Offer and action last

A “50% off” badge, the “Come for the sun, stay for the fun” line and a Book now button close the page.

Screens

The poster

The finished poster, presented in a beach mockup.

Outcome

What changed

  • A poster that moves from mood to facilities to booking in one glance
  • A Sunscape logo and a warm visual style that ties the page together
  • A clear offer and call to action
