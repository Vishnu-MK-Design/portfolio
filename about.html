What I believe

Design philosophy.

Four principles I come back to on every project.

01

Simplicity

Design should remove complexity, not add to it.

02

Clarity

People should never have to wonder what to do next.

03

Consistency

A product should feel familiar and predictable.

04

Accessibility

Good design should work for everyone.

Process

How I work.

A repeatable, evidence-led process that turns an unclear problem into a product people can use without having to think about it.

Step 01 / 05

Discover

Understanding users, stakeholders and business goals before any screens are drawn.

What I do
  • User interviews
  • Stakeholder goals
  • Competitor review
You get

A shared picture of who the product is for and what success looks like.

Step 02 / 05

Define

Finding the real problems worth solving — and deciding what to leave out.

What I do
  • User flows
  • Journey mapping
  • Problem framing
You get

Prioritised problems and flows the whole team agrees on.

Step 03 / 05

Design

Structure first, then visuals — from wireframes to a polished, systemised UI.

What I do
  • Wireframes
  • UI design
  • Design system
  • Prototypes
You get

Interactive prototypes and components that are ready to test.

Step 04 / 05

Test

Putting the design in front of real people and checking assumptions early.

What I do
  • Usability testing
  • Feedback sessions
  • Accessibility checks
You get

Evidence of what works, what confuses people, and why.

Step 05 / 05

Deliver & improve

Handing off cleanly, then iterating on what we learn after launch.

What I do
  • Developer handoff
  • Design QA
  • Iteration
You get

Build-ready specs and a clear list of improvements for the next cycle.
